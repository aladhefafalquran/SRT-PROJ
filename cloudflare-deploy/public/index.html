Why a third-party service?

Your browser can't fetch videos directly from YouTube/Instagram/TikTok — those sites block non-browser requests. cobalt is a free, open-source project that does the download and hands you a stream URL. The video is downloaded directly to your browser, not stored on cobalt's servers. If their API is down, you can also just use yt-dlp on your own machine and drag the result into the editor.
